Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own board?

No! All our lessons include high-quality soft-top boards perfect for learning, as well as wetsuits.

What if it rains?

Surfing is a water sport! We surf rain or shine, unless conditions are dangerous (lightning or extreme storm).

Is there an age limit?

We teach kids from age 6 and up. Swimming ability is required for safety.
