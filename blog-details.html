How to Read a Surf Forecast

Oct 12, 2024 • 5 Min Read

Understanding the ocean is the first step to becoming a better surfer. Here is everything you need to know about swell data.

1. Swell Height vs. Wave Height

Swell height refers to the size of the wave in open ocean. Breaking wave height depends on the beach topography.

2. Swell Period

The time in seconds between two wave crests. A longer period (10s+) usually means more power and cleaner waves.

"The wind is the brush that paints the ocean's surface. Ideally, you want no wind or offshore wind."

3. Wind Direction

Offshore wind blows from land to sea, grooming the waves. Onshore wind blows from sea to land, making them choppy.
