Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first lesson.

Do I need my own surfboard?

No! We provide high-quality soft-top boards perfect for learning, as well as wetsuits. Just bring your swimsuit and a towel.

What is the minimum age?

We teach kids as young as 5 years old in our private lessons, and 8 years old for group sessions. All children must be comfortable in the water.

What if the weather is bad?

Surfing is a water sport, so we surf in the rain! However, if conditions are unsafe (lightning or high winds), we will reschedule your lesson for free.
